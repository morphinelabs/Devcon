MUMBAI, NOT JUST THE VENUE

Devcon 8 takes place in Mumbai from November 3 to 6, 2026. The conference itself is one part of the trip. OnGround is here for everything you will remember outside it.

WHAT YOU CAN EXPECT

You can run in the morning, try Mumbai food during the day, and spend one evening celebrating India with food, lights and music.

The idea
Some of the best parts of a trip are the things you did not plan. That is what we want to bring to Mumbai.

You will have four days of talks, meetings and people to meet. We want the mornings and evenings around those days to feel different. You should be able to eat something you have never tried, see something you have never seen and spend time with people you would not have met inside a conference room.

FOR FIRST TIME VISITORS

We will make it easy to experience a few things that are genuinely local without asking you to spend days researching where to go.

FOR PEOPLE WHO KNOW INDIA

Bring your friends, take a break from the conference and enjoy a version of Mumbai that feels like a city rather than a conference stop.

What is happening

Three ways to experience Mumbai during Devcon week.

You can join one experience or all three, depending on what you feel like doing that day.

01 / MORNING

Mumbai Run Club

We will meet in the morning for an easy group run, then slow down over chai and breakfast. The route will be comfortable for visitors and the pace will be friendly.

We will run together and then sit down for chai and breakfast.
02 / DAYTIME

Mumbai Food Carts

We will bring small Mumbai food stops to approved locations around the places where people are staying and spending time, with vada pav, chai, pani puri, jalebi and more.

You can stop for something to eat and discover another side of Mumbai.
03 / EVENING

India Night

We will spend one evening celebrating India through food, Diwali lights, music, dhol, mehendi, colour and the energy of a traditional baraat.

We will eat together, listen to music and celebrate India.

Mumbai Run Club

You do not need to be a serious runner.

We are keeping this simple. Meet in the morning, run through a good part of Mumbai, stop for chai and breakfast, take a few photographs and head into the day. The route will be chosen with visitors in mind, and the pace will stay comfortable enough for conversation.

DISTANCE

PACE

The group will stay together rather than turning the morning into a race.

FINISH

Every run will end with chai and breakfast so people have time to sit down and talk.

Questions

A few things you may want to know.

When is this happening?

The experiences are built around Devcon week in Mumbai. Devcon 8 is taking place from November 3 to 6, 2026 at Jio World Centre in BKC.

Who can join?

Anyone visiting Mumbai that week is welcome to register interest. Some experiences will have limited capacity, and final details will be shared with people on the list.

Do I need a Devcon ticket?

No. The OnGround experiences are separate from Devcon. You can register your interest even if you are coming to Mumbai for the wider week rather than attending the conference itself.

Do I have to pay to join?

We will share the final details for each experience with the people on the list. The public page does not require payment to register interest.

Will I know the exact locations before I arrive?

Yes. Meeting points and final locations will be shared with registered guests once they are confirmed and approved for the relevant experience.
