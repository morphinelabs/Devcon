Questions

Before you come.

Is this an official Devcon event?

Devcon Diwali Night is planned as an independently organised event during Devcon week. It is not presented as an official Devcon event.

Do I need a Devcon ticket?

No. The event is separate from the conference. We are building the guest list around people visiting Mumbai during the week.

Where is the venue?

The final venue is being confirmed. It will be in Mumbai and the exact location will be shared with confirmed guests.

Is entry guaranteed after joining the list?

No. The event is capped at 350 guests. Joining the list registers your interest; final invitations will be confirmed later.
